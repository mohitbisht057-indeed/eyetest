Standard geometry: outer diameter subtends 5 arcminutes at 6/6; stroke and gap are each one-fifth of the outer diameter (1 arcminute at 6/6). Values are computed with S = 2D × tan(θ / 2), where D is viewing distance and θ is 5 arcminutes multiplied by the Snellen denominator / 6.
